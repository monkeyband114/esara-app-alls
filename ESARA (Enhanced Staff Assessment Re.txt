STAFF RESEARCH SCORE ALGORITHM
Calculate:
Publication Score = Publications / Max Publications
Citation Score = Citations / Max Citations
H Index Score = H Index / Max H Index
Journal Quality Score = Journal Quality / Max Journal Quality
SRS =
(0.25 × Publication Score)
+
(0.35 × Citation Score)
+
(0.25 × H Index Score)
+
(0.15 × Journal Quality Score)
Store all calculations.
Recompute automatically whenever publication data changes.

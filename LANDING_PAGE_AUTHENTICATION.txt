Find matching authors.

Step 3:

Calculate matching confidence based on:

Full Name

Institution

Research Area

Select highest-confidence match.

Step 5:

Import:

Publications

Citation Counts

h-index

Institution Information

# PUBLICATION CLAIMING SYSTEM

Sometimes publications may not be automatically matched.

Create a Claim Publication workflow.

Users can:

Search OpenAlex

Select publication

Submit claim

System verifies:

Author Name Similarity

Institution Similarity

Research Area Similarity

Publication Metadata

If confidence exceeds threshold:

Automatically approve.

Otherwise:

Send to ICT Personnel Verification Queue.
